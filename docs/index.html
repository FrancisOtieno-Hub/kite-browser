What's in the box

Everything a browser
should already do

Tracker & ad blocking

On by default, with a per-site allow toggle when a page needs it — right from the shield badge in the toolbar.

Password vault

One master password unlocks everything else. Save, autofill, and manage logins without a third-party extension.

Genuine private tabs

Backed by the OS's own incognito profile — real isolation, marked with its own violet tint right in your normal window.

Fast & light

No second browser engine shipped inside the app — Kite stays out of your RAM's way.

Open source

The full source is on GitHub. Read it, build it yourself, or send a fix.

Tabs that stay put

Session restore, favicons, a bookmarks bar, history and downloads — the basics, done properly.

Built-in extensions

Seven extensions, opt-in and ready to switch on: dark mode, cosmetic ad blocking, SponsorBlock, YouTube ad skipping, a volume booster, reader mode, and Vimium-style keyboard navigation.
